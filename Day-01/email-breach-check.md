 #Day 1 — Email Breach Awareness

What I did

I learned about email data breaches and used Have I Been Pwned
to check whether an email address had appeared in known data breaches.


What I learned

- What a data breach is
- What compromised credentials mean
- Why reused passwords can be dangerous
- How breach-checking services can help users become aware of
  exposed information

## Security lesson

A breach does not necessarily mean that the person's current
password is compromised. It means that the email/account identifier
appeared in data from a known breach.

## What I learned today

I understood that cybersecurity is not only about hacking.
It also involves awareness, prevention, monitoring, governance,
risk management and incident response.
